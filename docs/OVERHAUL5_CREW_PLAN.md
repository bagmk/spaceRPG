## 0. 왜 (fun-audit 진단 요약)

- 아이템 294개가 기계적으로 ~25버킷(등급×효과타입, 버킷 내 수치 동일)으로 붕괴 — 드랍에 서프라이즈·결정 없음 (판정단 4/4 심각도 5).
- 캐릭터/애착 앵커 전무 — 프레스티지 후 감정적으로 이어지는 것 없음.
- 스테이지 4–15가 동일 21아이템 템플릿 복붙 — 새 스테이지에 새 콘텐츠 없음.
- 큰 순간(신화 융합·스테이지 클리어·프레스티지) 무연출.

### 비평 반영 확정 (구현 전 잠금)

1. **스테이지 17 정책**: 코드에는 stage 17(신화 5종: Singularity Core 등)이 실존(총 294 확인). 크루는 뽑지 않고 **최상급 카드 5종**(엔드게임 승급 연료·수집 왕관)으로 남긴다. 로스터는 정확히 50 유지.
2. **로스터 버그 수정 완료**: s10_01(실제=Sun)→**s10_02**(Dust Grain)로 교정, s9_09(AGN, 소스 effect=auto)는 role **rift**로 교정 → 최종 role 분포 click 20 / rift 16 / wild 14.
3. **티어→파워 분기(핵심)**: 크루 파워는 `crew[id].tier`가 결정 — effects.ts에서 크루 슬롯이면 ENTITY_RARITY_EFFECT_SCALE/SECONDARY_RARITY_*를 **crew.tier로 인덱싱**(정적 entity.rarity 아님). 정적 경로 재사용만 하면 조용히 무효(no-op)됨 — 명시적 코드 포크 필수. 심 검증은 crew-tier-aware 모드를 심에 추가한 뒤 수행(Phase 2).
4. **합류 = 스테이지 진입 시 자동(무료)**: 각 크루는 joinStage 도달 시 자동 합류(대사와 함께, 스테이지당 2~4명 = 매 스테이지 새 콘텐츠 비트). buy-to-recruit 아님. 드랍은 순수 카드만.
5. **마이그레이션 시딩(v32→v33)**: 크루 tier = 소스 엔티티의 정적 rarity(승급 개념이 없었으므로 유일 정답), level = 보유 최고레벨 카피, unlocked = (카피 보유 ‖ 현재 스테이지 ≥ joinStage). 여분 카피 → 카드 1장/카피 + 레벨 환급(**CARD_ENHANCE_REFUND: 여분 카피의 (level−1)당 강화석 1개**).
6. **천장(pity)**: v1은 **전역 공유**(기존 fusionsSinceMythic 카운터를 promoteAttemptsSinceMythic으로 개명·재사용). per-crew는 플레이테스트 후 재검토.
7. **same-entity 보너스 → same-subset 보너스**: 크루 id가 카드 풀에서 빠지므로 승급 성공률 보너스는 같은 코덱스 서브셋 카드 기준으로 재정의.
8. **favoriteEntityIds**: 크루 id를 가리키는 기존 즐겨찾기는 필드에 남기되 UI에서 무시(무해).
9. **quality/carried**: 카드는 수량 스택(퍼카피 quality 소멸 — 파워상 이미 죽은 스탯). 관련 UI는 Phase 4에서 제거.

### 퍽 패밀리 밸런스 경고 (비평가)
- No two crew share an IDENTICAL perk shape (verified: all three docs' own design-notes lists claim distinct mechanical hooks and manual review of all 50 perk descriptions found no exact duplicates), BUT there are near-duplicate/overlapping perk FAMILIES worth flagging for balance review: (1) drop-rate perks cluster — Top Quark (click-drop-rate), Cosmic Transparency (global drop-rate), Stellar Mass Black Hole (auto-source drop-rate), Pop III Supernova (fusion-attempt card-drop-chance) — four separate drop-rate modifiers of increasing scope/overlap that could stack into a single dominant 'drop rate' build if all equipped simultaneously; no doc caps or diminishing-returns this stack. (2) comet-perk cluster — Inflaton Surge (comet payout size), 21cm Signal (comet frequency), Comet itself (both payout AND frequency per roster-mid's own description), Final Evaporation Flash (comet power +25%) — four comet-boosting crew whose combined effect on the comet mechanic (already flagged in memory as an area needing pacing care) has not been sim-checked. (3) offline-rate cluster — False Vacuum Bubble (flat offline-rate), Cold Hydrogen (tiered long-idle offline bonus), Neutron Star (+15% offline efficiency) — three, with unclear stacking order/interaction rules specified nowhere.

### 경제 리스크
- 12% base + N=40 hard pity on Legendary→Mythic could feel worse than the existing fusion path if players compare per-attempt odds directly (fusion's 0.03 looks lower but fusion is cheap/repeatable per-trio; promotion's 40-card cost per attempt makes each roll expensive) — playtest actual attempts-per-hour before launch, not just theoretical EV.
- Reusing a SHARED global pity counter for all 50 crew's Legendary→Mythic attempts (rather than per-crew) means a lucky early success for one crew consumes/resets the counter for everyone else's push — this could feel bad ('I was so close and someone else's promotion ate my pity'); consider per-crew pity if playtesting shows frustration, at the cost of the sim-neutral 'reuse FUSION_MYTHIC_PITY_N as-is' simplicity.
- Card era-window (±2/±3 stages) combined with subset-fraction minimums may create a farming bottleneck for crew whose home stage is very early (stage 1-2) or very late (stage 15-16) since the window has fewer neighboring stages to draw from — needs an edge-case rule (e.g. clamp window to available stages and lower the subset-fraction minimum proportionally) or those crew become unfarmable-cheap or unfarmable-hard outliers.
- 244 codex cards across 16 stages is ~15/stage; a single crew promotion push (up to 40 cards for Legendary→Mythic) could visibly drain one stage's card pool if the player is promoting multiple same-era crew simultaneously — verify aggregate demand (50 crew × their lifetime card costs) against total realistic card-drop supply per stage before locking constants.
- Crew surviving prestige while cards/stones reset means a prestige-heavy player's crew roster could 'cap out' at whatever tier was reachable pre-prestige and then stay there for many runs since re-promotion requires refarming the full card cost from zero each time — may unintentionally discourage prestiging once a satisfying crew tier is reached; consider a small prestige-carry discount on card costs (e.g. -10% per prestige, capped) if this shows up as a retention risk.
- The epic-tier soft-pity (+5%/fail, cap +25%, per-crew) needs a UI surface (a visible pity meter) or players won't perceive it and will feel the 40% base rate is worse than it is — same UX debt the existing fusion pity already has; don't ship silently.
